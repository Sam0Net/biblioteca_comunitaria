## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a warm, botanical-editorial, community-first ethos. Built to foster connection, quiet focus, and shared cultural stewardship, the visual tone blends the earthy serenity of a sunlit neighborhood reading garden with the disciplined readability of classical library publishing.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
The audience spans community neighbors, local students, cultural event attendees, families, and volunteer donors. The interface evokes:
- **Welcoming Calm:** Removing bureaucratic barriers to borrowing books, reserving community rooms, or signing up for cultural workshops.
- **Organic Growth:** Celebrating books as living seeds of community wisdom, reflected in botanical motifs, deep chlorophyll hues, and tactile parchment textures.
- **Approachable Craft:** Soft, rounded curves combined with crystal-clear typographic hierarchies that feel hand-curated rather than corporate.

### Design Movement: Warm Botanical Editorial
The visual signature combines modern flat surfaces, generous negative space, gentle organic curves (up to 32px radii), and parchment surfaces. Rather than sharp tech lines or stark cold grays, it employs sage containers, deep forest pine text anchors, and warm linen-tinted neutrals.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from classic book bindings, living foliage, and unbleached rag paper. 

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#1a4d3b` - Forest Deep):** Used for primary calls-to-action, navigation headers, structural brand icons, table headings, and prominent section titles. Signals natural gravitas and focused reading.
- **Secondary (`#d4e6dd` - Sage Soft):** Used as the foundational surface tint for cards, asides, badges, and table highlighting. Provides an airy botanical background without visual fatigue.
- **Tertiary (`#c48849` - Warm Amber):** Evokes aged leather book spines, bookmark ribbons, and sunlit reading nooks. Reserved for star ratings, availability warnings, special event badges, and donation milestones.
- **Neutral (`#f4efe6` - Parchment Base):** Soft paper ground for application scaffolds, scroll tracks, and subtle divider areas.

### Availability & Status Tones
- **Available / En Estantería:** `#2b6e4e` with `#e8f3ee` background badge.
- **Borrowed / En Préstamo:** `#b45309` with `#fef3c7` background badge.
- **Reserved / Sala Ocupada:** `#2f5c78` with `#e0f2fe` background badge.

### Contrast & Legibility Rules
- High-contrast ink text (`#111111` or `#1a4d3b`) is strictly maintained on all lighter grounds (`#ffffff`, `#fbf9f5`, `#f4efe6`, `#d4e6dd`).
- When text is placed on primary Forest Deep buttons or banners, pure white (`#ffffff`) is applied with minimum font weight of 500.

## Typography

The typographic pairing marries the friendly, organic warmth of **Fredoka** for headings, brand signatures, and badges with the crystalline clarity and open counters of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for catalog tables, book synopses, and form inputs.

### Font Roles & Pairings
- **Headings & Badges (Fredoka):** Generous rounded terminals soften formal library administration, making exploration welcoming to children, elders, and all neighborhood patrons.
- **Body & Tabular Reading (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Ensures extended reading comfort across catalog matrices, bibliographic metadata (ISBN, shelf location, publication date), and cultural calendar listings.
- **Book Titles:** Styled with medium or semi-bold weight and gentle italic accents in catalog records to preserve classic bibliographic tradition while maintaining digital clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column asymmetric grid that accommodates both a functional sidebar index (3 columns on desktop) and a fluid, content-rich operational canvas (9 columns).

### Breakpoints & Adaptability
- **Desktop (`≥ 1000px`):** 12-column layout. The left aside (3 columns, minimum 280px) remains sticky at `top: 2.5rem` for quick jumping between *Catálogo de Libros*, *Salas de Lectura*, *Actividades Culturales*, and *Donaciones*. The main canvas spans the remaining 9 columns with a `2.5rem` gap.
- **Tablet (`600px - 999px`):** The sidebar collapses into an expansive horizontal category pill bar with scroll snap. Main content spans full viewport minus `2rem` outer padding.
- **Mobile (`< 600px`):** Single-column stacked rhythm. Form controls, catalog cards, and filter triggers span 100% width. Padding standardizes to `1rem` to preserve screen real estate. Tables activate smooth horizontal panning with sticky left book titles.

### Spacing Cadence
- Content sections inside the main workspace stack with `space-4xl` (4rem / 64px) or `space-5xl` (5rem / 80px) margins to preserve tranquility and prevent cognitive clutter.
- Card padding remains uniformly comfortable: `space-xl` (2rem / 32px) for desktop containers, reducing to `space-md` (1rem / 16px) on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews heavy artificial drop shadows in favor of **tinted tonal nesting** and **organic layering**. Depth is conveyed through parchment-and-leaf layering, referencing physical books resting on clean oak desks.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Floor Ground):** Base page parchment fill (`#f4efe6`).
- **Level 1 (Subtle Inset / Panels):** Alternating table rows and quiet card wells using `#fbf9f5` or light sage `#e8f3ee`. Border stroke: `1px solid rgba(26, 77, 59, 0.15)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Containers & Modules):** Main sidebar, service cards, and modal dialogs using `#d4e6dd` or crisp `#ffffff`. These sit flat with soft 1px organic forest borders (`rgba(26, 77, 59, 0.2)`).
- **Level 3 (Interactive Floating Accents):** Hero media cards and active dialogs use a diffused botanical shadow: `0 20px 45px -10px rgba(26, 77, 59, 0.18)`.
- **Level 4 (Focus States):** Input fields, radio items, and buttons receive a gentle glowing halo: `0 0 0 4px rgba(26, 77, 59, 0.15)` paired with a crisp `1.5px solid #1a4d3b` border.

### Decorative Depth Overlays
Playful botanical vector silhouettes (leaves, sprouting stems) are positioned behind content cards with low opacities (`0.25` - `0.35`) and rotational offsets (`-12deg` and `+45deg`), establishing tactile depth without interfering with screen reader parsing or visual legibility.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses an organic, pebble-like smoothness. Structural items balance accessibility with rounded warmth.

### Corner Radius Mapping
- **Pill / Circular (`9999px`):** Primary action buttons (`.btn-principal`), filter badges, status tags, author chips, and numbered step markers.
- **Jumbo Shell (`32px` / `2rem`):** Major content modules including sidebar index boxes, workshop detail panels, and room reservation cards.
- **Container Medium (`16px` / `1rem`):** Book cover wrappers, catalog tables, and interactive calendar day containers.
- **Control Soft (`12px` / `0.75rem`):** Form inputs, text areas, search fields, and secondary control buttons.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Reservar, Buscar, Donar):** Pill-shaped (`9999px` radius), background `#1a4d3b`, label `#ffffff` in Fredoka SemiBold. Padding `0.85rem 2rem`. On hover: lifts slightly with `transform: translateY(-2px)` and darkens to `#123b2d`.
- **Secondary (Ver Detalles, Cancelar):** Background `#d4e6dd`, text `#1a4d3b`. On hover: background `#c5dbcf`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, `1.5px solid rgba(26, 77, 59, 0.3)`, text `#1a4d3b`.

### 2. Catalog Table & Book Items
- **Header:** Background `#1a4d3b`, text `#ffffff` with rounded top corners (`16px`).
- **Data Rows:** Alternating row fills between `#ffffff` and `#fbf9f5`. Hover state applies `rgba(212, 230, 221, 0.45)` with immediate row highlight.
- **Book Cover Display:** Aspect ratio 2:3, border radius `10px`, subtle spine shadow along the left edge (`inset 4px 0 6px -2px rgba(0,0,0,0.3)`).

### 3. Status Badges & Chips
- **Availability Chips:** Compact pill layout with a leading 6px colored indicator dot.
  - *Disponible:* Sage green fill `#e8f3ee`, text `#1a4d3b`, dot `#2b6e4e`.
  - *En Préstamo:* Warm cream fill `#fef3c7`, text `#92400e`, dot `#b45309`.
  - *En Reparación / Taller:* Light slate fill `#f1f5f9`, text `#475569`, dot `#64748b`.
- **Genre & Category Filters:** Interactive pills. Unselected: `#ffffff` with `1px solid rgba(26,77,59,0.2)`. Selected: `#1a4d3b` with white text.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Search Catalog Input:** Deep rounded (`9999px` or `12px`), background `#ffffff`, border `1.5px solid rgba(26, 77, 59, 0.25)`. Leading leaf or magnifying glass icon in `#1a4d3b`.
- **Form Textarea & Select:** Radius `12px`, padding `0.85rem 1rem`. Focus state triggers the botanical halo: `0 0 0 4px rgba(26, 77, 59, 0.15)`.

### 5. Community Room & Event Cards
- **Card Container:** Background `#ffffff` or `#d4e6dd`, border-radius `32px`, padding `2rem`.
- **Card Elements:** Includes event date marker (styled as an open book page), attendee capacity bar, and "Reservar Sala" trigger.
- **Donation Tracker:** Visual seed-to-tree progress bar filled with `#1a4d3b` against a `#e8f3ee` track, celebrating community contributions.
